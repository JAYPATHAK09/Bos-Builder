# M01: PURPOSE & VALUES MODULE PROMPT
## Generate Foundational Identity Framework for Company

**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{MODULE_INPUTS}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

Purpose and Values define WHO the company is beyond the business model. They answer:
- Why does this company exist? (Purpose)
- What does it stand for? (Values)
- What will it never compromise on? (Non-negotiables)

These become reference points for hiring, performance management, decision-making, and culture. 
They must be specific enough to guide behavior, not generic aspirations.

---

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. PURPOSE STATEMENT (1-2 sentences, 30-50 words)

The Purpose answers: "Why does this company exist beyond making profit?"

**Criteria**:
- Specific to this company's domain and impact
- Inspirational but realistic
- Testable (would team members recognize it in daily decisions?)
- Distinct from mission or vision
- Contains an element of impact/contribution

**Common Mistakes to Avoid**:
- Vague ("We enable transformation") - too generic
- Only financial ("Maximize shareholder returns") - not inspiring
- Too narrow ("Sell widgets") - not a purpose, a transaction
- Conflating with vision ("Become the market leader") - is about position, not why

**Question for Consultant**: What problem does this company solve that the founder is personally 
passionate about? What would the founder do even if it were less profitable?

---

### 2. CORE VALUES (3-6 values, depth over breadth)

Each value has three components:

**a) Value Name** (one word, max two)
- Examples: Integrity, Ownership, Excellence, Customer-First, Transparency

**b) 1-Line Definition** (15-30 words)
- What does this value mean in this company's context?
- Specific enough that two people could interpret similarly

**c) 3 Behavioral Indicators** (observable actions, not traits)
- How would you recognize this value in someone's behavior?
- Phrased as "Does X" or "Always/Never does Y"
- Actionable for hiring and performance reviews
- Example: 
  - Value: "Ownership"
  - Indicator 1: "Raises hand for problems, even outside their role"
  - Indicator 2: "Drives solutions to completion; doesn't blame others for delays"
  - Indicator 3: "Proactively communicates blockers and impact; doesn't wait to be asked"

**Consultant Checklist for Values**:
- [ ] Each value is specific to this company's context (not generic)
- [ ] Values are not contradictory (if two seem opposed, explore nuance)
- [ ] Behavioral indicators are observable and testable
- [ ] Values align with founder/leadership's actual behavior (not aspirational PR)
- [ ] Maximum 6 values (fewer is better; focus is critical)
- [ ] Each value is defensible; team would fight for it

---

### 3. NON-NEGOTIABLES (2-4 items)

Non-negotiables are boundaries this company will never cross, even under pressure.

**Examples**:
- We will never compromise data security to save costs
- We will never pressure sales team to sell to unsuitable clients
- We will never communicate dishonestly to investors or customers
- We will never skip quality gates in production

**Phrasing**: "We will never [negative] because [consequence/principle]"

**Difference from Values**:
- Values are aspirational
- Non-negotiables are defensive (lines we won't cross)

---

## INPUT INJECTION TEMPLATE

Replace {{COMPANY_PROFILE}} with:
- Company name, age, stage (Seed/Series A/B/Profitable)
- Industry vertical, business model (B2B/SaaS/D2C/Services)
- Founder background and passion (what problem did they see?)
- Current team size and culture observations
- Growth stage challenges (scaling? pivoting?)

Replace {{MODULE_INPUTS}} with:
- Any pre-existing value statements or cultural artifacts
- Founder's personal mission statement (if available)
- Customer feedback on what they love about the company
- What values have shown up naturally in hiring/promotions?

## How to Use This Framework

- **Hiring**: Interview questions should probe for alignment with values
- **Performance Management**: Link behavioral indicators to performance ratings (M08)
- **Decision-Making**: When conflicted, ask "Which value applies here?"
- **Culture**: Reinforce through stories, recognition, corrective action
