# M03: 3-YEAR STRATEGIC PLAN MODULE PROMPT
## Translate Vision into 3-Year Roadmap with Strategic Themes and Targets

**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{MODULE_INPUTS}}, {{UPSTREAM_M02}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The 3-Year Strategic Plan bridges the 10-year Vision to the 1-year Annual Operating Plan. 
It answers:
- What are the 3-5 strategic priorities for the next 3 years?
- What does success look like in Year 1, 2, 3? (Revenue, scale, product, market expansion)
- What major initiatives will we fund to achieve those goals?
- What are the risks, and how do we mitigate them?

This plan is a "working document" that gets refreshed annually as you learn.

---

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. STRATEGIC THEMES (3-5 themes with rationale)

Each theme is a major area of focus. Themes typically fall into categories:
- Market Expansion (new geographies, segments, channels)
- Product / Service (new offerings, deepening existing)
- Operational Excellence (efficiency, automation, quality)
- Capability Building (talent, technology, partnerships)
- Customer Retention / Expansion (NRR, upsell, retention)

**Template for Each Theme**:

**Theme Name**: [Clear name, e.g., "Expand into SME Segment", "Build AI-Powered Core"]

**Rationale**: 
- Why now? (market opportunity, competitive threat, internal capability)
- Strategic impact (how does this move us toward Vision?)
- Success metric (how will we know we nailed this theme in 3 years?)

**Example Theme**:

**Theme**: "Scale from B2B Services to B2B SaaS Product"
**Rationale**: Market is shifting toward self-serve software. We have proven demand from 20+ services clients. 
Building a product enables 10x scale without proportional headcount. Takes us closer to Vision of 
"10,000 customers using our platform."
**Success Metric (Y3)**: 500+ SaaS customers, $2M ARR, 60%+ NRR

---

### 2. REVENUE TARGETS BY YEAR

Provide Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 targets broken down by revenue stream (if >1 stream exists).

**Format**:

| Revenue Stream | Y0 (Actual) | Y1 Target | Y1 Rationale | Y2 Target | Y3 Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core Service | ₹XX Cr | ₹XX Cr | [growth driver: new clients/pricing/delivery] | ₹XX Cr | ₹XX Cr |
| Product Revenue | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr | [launch in Q2] | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr |
| **Total** | **₹XX Cr** | **₹XX Cr** | | **₹XX Cr** | **₹XX Cr** |

**Guidance on Target-Setting**:
- Year 1: Conservative (80% confidence achievable). Builds credibility.
- Year 2-3: Progressive stretch (60% confidence). Challenging but motivating.
- Consider: current growth rate, market size, sales capacity, product readiness
- Always include rationale (not just a number)

---

### 3. KEY INITIATIVES PER THEME (3-5 initiatives per theme)

Initiatives are major projects that deliver the theme.

**Template**:

| Initiative | Theme | Owner | Timeline | Investment | Key Milestone (Y1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [Theme] | [Role] | Q1-Q2 | ₹[XX] | [Deliverable in Year 1] |

**Example Initiatives** for "Expand to SME Segment" theme:

| Initiative | Theme | Owner | Timeline | Investment | Y1 Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build Channel Partner Program | Expand SME | VP Sales | Q2-Q4 | ₹20L | 10 active partners, 50 SME customers acquired |
| Launch Product-Lite Offering | Expand SME | Product | Q1-Q3 | ₹50L | SME-specific feature set, 2 beta customers |
| Create SME-Focused Marketing | Expand SME | Marketing | Q1-Q2 | ₹15L | 10K website visitors/month, 5% conversion to trial |

---

### 4. KEY RISKS PER THEME (2-3 risks per theme)

Identify what could derail each theme and mitigation.

**Format**:

| Theme | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Theme] | [Risk: e.g., key person departure] | H/M/L | H/M/L | [Action: e.g., document processes, identify successor] |

**Example**:

| Theme | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scale SaaS | Rapid churn due to poor onboarding | M | H | Hire Customer Success manager by Q2; build onboarding checklist; measure NRR monthly |

---

### 5. RESOURCE & INVESTMENT INDICATIVE

For each major initiative, estimate investment needed (headcount, budget, time).

**Format** (Inline or Summary Table):

- **Headcount**: X new roles needed (e.g., 2 engineers, 1 product manager)
- **Budget**: ₹XX Cr / ₹XX L in FY2025-26
- **Existing Capacity**: Y% of current team re-allocated to these initiatives

**Consultant Insight**: If sum of initiatives exceeds 100% of org capacity, you've over-committed. 
Prioritize ruthlessly.

---

### 6. YEAR-1 HEADLINE PLANNING BRIDGE

The Year-1 specific targets and initiatives become inputs to the Annual Operating Plan (M04). 
Provide a summary that sets up AOP:

**Year 1 Success Looks Like**:
- Revenue target: ₹XX Cr
- Customer base: X customers (new: Y)
- Product milestones: [List 3-5 major product launches/updates]
- Org scale: X people → X people
- Market presence: [Geographic, segment, or channel expansion]
