## Define Organizational Structure, Roles, and Competency Framework

**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{UPSTREAM_M01}}, {{UPSTREAM_M05}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The Org Chart defines WHO does WHAT and for WHOM. It creates:
- **Org Structure**: Visual hierarchy and reporting lines
- **Role Inventory**: Complete list of roles, levels, and departments
- **Job Descriptions**: Purpose, responsibilities, qualifications, competencies for each role
- **KPI Ownership**: Which role owns which KPI (from M05)
- **Competency Framework**: Link between company values (M01) and required skills
- **Succession Plan**: How organization scales as company grows

A well-designed org structure:
- **Clear Accountability**: No role ambiguity; each person knows their scope
- **Appropriate Span of Control**: Managers have 4-8 direct reports (healthy range)
- **Values-Aligned**: Roles reflect and reinforce company values
- **KPI-Linked**: Each role has clear KPI owners and targets
- **Growth-Ready**: Template for future expansion (e.g., "when we hit 50 people, hire 2 more engineers")

### 3. JOB DESCRIPTION TEMPLATE (For Each Role)

Create detailed job descriptions that are actionable and align to values/KPIs.

**Template** (One per role):

### [ROLE: Account Executive]

**Department**: Sales | **Reports To**: VP Sales | **Level**: L3

**Role Purpose** (1 sentence):
Acquire and onboard new customers to achieve ARR targets while ensuring fit and customer success.

**Key Responsibilities** (8-10 bullet points):
1. **Target Pursuit**: Manage assigned account list and pipeline toward annual new ARR quota (₹50L)
2. **Discovery & Qualification**: Conduct needs analysis; qualify inbound and SDR-sourced leads; estimate deal value
3. **Proposal & Negotiation**: Present solutions; negotiate terms; handle objections; drive to close
4. **Deal Management**: Track deal progress in CRM; provide weekly forecast updates; escalate blockers
5. **Onboarding**: Hand-off to Customer Success; ensure customer success setup and initial training
6. **Account Growth**: Identify expansion opportunities within existing accounts; sell add-ons/upgrades
7. **Sales Methodology**: Follow company sales process; update CRM daily; attend sales training and coaching
8. **Collaboration**: Coordinate with Product on feature requests; with CS on customer success; with Finance on contracts
9. **Market Feedback**: Provide customer insights to Product and Leadership on use cases, objections, market trends
10. **Professional Development**: Participate in quarterly coaching and training; develop sales skills

**Key Performance Indicators** (Link to M05):
- **New ARR**: ₹50L annual target (₹4.17L/month average); cascaded from company target ₹100 Cr
- **Expansion ARR**: ₹10L annual target (sell into existing customers)
- **Pipeline Coverage**: Maintain 3x monthly quota (₹12.5L ongoing pipeline)
- **Win Rate**: 25-30% (of proposals)
- **Sales Cycle**: 40-50 days (from first meeting to close)
- **CSAT**: 4.5/5 from customers you've closed

**Required Qualifications**:
- 3+ years of B2B SaaS sales experience (or equivalent B2B
- Proven track record of quota achievement (80%+ hit rate)
- Experience with CRM systems (Salesforce or equivalent)
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent sales experience

**Preferred Qualifications**:
- Enterprise software sales experience
- Experience selling to [TARGET CUSTOMER TYPE]
- MBA or advanced sales certification

**Competencies & Values Alignment** (Link to M01):

| Company Value | Behavioral Indicator | Assessment in Role | How It Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer-First** | Prioritizes customer success over commission | Never pressure customer into unsuitable deal | Ask solution fit questions before pricing |
| **Ownership** | Drives deals to closure; owns pipeline | Completes all pipeline activities; doesn't blame marketing | Updates CRM daily; proactive follow-ups |
| **Collaboration** | Works cross-functionally with CS and Product | Solid handoff to CS team; provides customer feedback | Weekly sync with CS team; monthly product feedback |
| **Excellence** | Strives for mastery; high professional standards | Continuously improves sales skills; keeps learning | Attends training; tries new sales techniques |
| **Integrity** | Never misrepresents product to close deals | Honest in conversations; discloses limitations | Candid about product gaps; recommends competitors if better fit |

**Reporting & Review**:
- Reports to VP Sales (weekly 1:1s)
- Reviewed in semi-annual performance review (M08)
- Bonus eligible: 20% of base salary (linked to New ARR + Expansion KPIs)

**Compensation & Benefits** (typical):
- Base Salary: ₹12-15L (depending on experience)
- Variable Bonus: 20% of base (up to ₹2.4-3L at 100% of targets)
- Total Comp Range: ₹14.4-18L

### 4. KPI OWNERSHIP MATRIX (Which Role Owns Which KPI)

Map M05 KPIs to roles for accountability.

**Format**:

| KPI | Owner Role | Secondary (Supports) | Frequency | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Revenue (MRR) | VP Sales | All Sales team | Weekly | ₹8.3 Cr | Rolled up from individual quotas |
| New Customer Count | VP Sales / AE | Marketing (sourcing) | Weekly | 50/month | Individual AE quotas sum to team target |
| Pipeline | VP Sales | AE (pipeline owners) | Weekly | 1.5x MRR | Updated per deal closed |
| NRR % | VP CS | CS team (per cohort) | Monthly | 110% | Cohort-level target |
| Churn Rate | VP CS | CS team (account management) | Monthly | 5% | Gross churn target |
| Gross Margin % | CFO | VP Eng (COGS), VP Ops | Monthly | 65% | Engineering headcount impacts margin |
| Feature Velocity | VP Eng | Engineering team | Monthly | 6 features/month | Weighted by complexity |
| Bug Severity | VP Eng | QA + Engineering | Weekly | <5 critical bugs | Quality metric |
| Headcount | CFO | HR | Monthly | 50 FTE (current: 20) | Hiring plan tracking |
| Cash Runway | CFO | Finance team | Weekly | >12 months | Critical metric |

**Accountability Rule**: Each KPI has ONE owner (not "owned by committee"). The owner is responsible for:
- Achieving the target (or explaining why not)
- Providing weekly/monthly updates
- Identifying blockers and requesting help
- Correcting course if off track

### 5. COMPETENCY FRAMEWORK (Map Values to Required Skills)

Define the skills and behaviors required for each role.

**Framework**:

| Company Value | Definition | Key Competencies | How Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer-First** | Prioritizes customer success over commission | Problem-Solving, Listening, Empathy, Solutions Thinking | Customer feedback, peer review, customer CSAT scores |
| **Ownership** | Drives outcomes; doesn't make excuses | Accountability, Initiative, Execution, Follow-through | KPI achievement, manager assessment, peer feedback |
| **Collaboration** | Works cross-functionally; elevates team | Communication, Teamwork, Trust, Influence | 360 feedback, cross-dept interviews, team feedback |
| **Excellence** | Strives for mastery; high standards | Attention to Detail, Continuous Learning, Ambition | Quality of work, training participation, self-assessment |
| **Integrity** | Does the right thing, even when hard | Ethics, Transparency, Honesty, Reliability | Ethical incident rate (zero), transparency in communication |

**Competency Levels** (for each role):

| Competency | Level 1 (IC) | Level 3 (Manager) | Level 5 (Director) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accountability** | Owns individual tasks; hits personal targets | Owns team outcomes; drives accountability in team | Owns function results; builds accountability culture |
| **Communication** | Clear in 1:1 and small groups | Articulate in meetings; leads presentations | Influences board and external stakeholders |
| **Strategic Thinking** | Understands role in strategy | Contributes to dept strategy | Shapes company strategy |
