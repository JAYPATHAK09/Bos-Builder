## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The MIS (Management Information System) Dashboard is the "control center" for monitoring KPIs in 
real-time. It translates the KPI Framework (M05) into visual, actionable dashboards.

An MIS Dashboard answers:
- What is the top-line health of the business RIGHT NOW?
- Which departments are on track vs. at risk?
- What actions do we need to take this week/month?
- How are we trending vs. plan?

A well-designed dashboard is:
- **Visual**: Use color (green/yellow/red RAG status), charts, sparklines, not just numbers
- **Automated**: No manual data entry; pulls from source systems in real-time
- **Hierarchical**: CEO dashboard shows top metrics; drill-down to department dashboards
- **Actionable**: Every metric has a clear owner and "if red, then we do X"

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. CEO DASHBOARD (Top 7 Metrics + Layout)

The CEO dashboard is reviewed daily/weekly and shown in investor updates and board meetings.

**Typical Layout**:
- Row 1 (Top): 3 critical metrics (revenue, margin, cash)
- Row 2 (Middle): 2-3 health metrics (churn, customer count, headcount)
- Row 3 (Bottom): Optional secondary metrics (pipeline, feature velocity)

**Metric Card Components** (for each metric):
- **Metric Name**: "Monthly Recurring Revenue"
- **Current Value**: ₹X Cr (updated daily/weekly)
- **Target Value**: ₹X Cr (from M04/M05)
- **% of Plan**: 98% (if 98% of target, flag yellow)
- **RAG Status**: Green / Yellow / Red
- **Trend**: Mini sparkline or % change MoM
- **Owner**: Name or role (clickable to drill-down)

**Example CEO Dashboard** (SaaS):

| Metric | Current | Target | % Plan | RAG | Trend | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | ₹5.2 Cr | ₹5.5 Cr | 95% | Yellow | ↑ 3% MoM | VP Sales |
| Gross Margin % | 62% | 65% | 95% | Yellow | ↓ 2% (eng costs up) | CFO |
| Net Revenue Retention | 108% | 110% | 98% | Green | Stable | VP CS |
| New Customer Count (Month) | 38 | 50 | 76% | Red | ↓ 15% vs last month | VP Sales |
| Cash Runway | 24 months | >12 months | 200% | Green | Stable | CFO |
| Headcount | 48 | 50 | 96% | Green | Hiring plan on track | HR |
| Feature Completion | 4/6 features | 6/6 | 67% | Yellow | 2 blocked on API | VP Eng |

**Color Rules** (Red/Yellow/Green):
- **Green**: >90% of target
- **Yellow**: 75-90% of target (watch list)
- **Red**: <75% of target (requires action)
- **Variance**: Show prior month/quarter for trend

### 3. METRIC-TO-SOURCE MAPPING TABLE

Document how each metric is calculated and which system owns the data.

**Format**:

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Data Source | Refresh Frequency | Owner | Calculation Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly ARR | Monthly recurring revenue from subscriptions | Sum of active monthly subs + annual / 12 | Stripe API + Accounting System | Daily | CFO | Update at 2 AM IST daily |
| New Customers | Count of new customers added in period | Count of contracts signed in month | CRM (Salesforce) | Daily | Sales Ops | Exclude trials, count as customer on contract sign |
| NRR % | Net revenue retention by cohort | (Revenue this month / revenue 12 months ago) * 100 | Accounting + Cohort analysis | Monthly (15th) | VP CS | Run monthly cohort analysis by acquisition month |
| Churn Rate | % of customers lost in month | (Customers lost this month / customers start of month) * 100 | CS Platform (Gainsight) | Daily | VP CS | Exclude voluntary downgrades to lower tier |

**Key Elements**:
- **Data Source**: Which system (Salesforce, Stripe, Google Analytics, etc.)
- **Refresh Frequency**: Daily/Weekly/Monthly (must be automated)
- **Owner**: Named role responsible for data integrity
- **Calculation Notes**: Any nuances (exclusions, adjustments, one-time items)

### 4. REFRESH CADENCES (Automation Schedule)

Define when each dashboard updates and how (automated vs. manual).

**Format**:

| Dashboard | Metrics | Refresh Schedule | Automation Tool | Manual Steps | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CEO Dashboard | Revenue, Margin, Churn, CAC, Headcount | Daily (8 AM IST) | Tableau / Data Studio | 0 (fully automated) | 99.5% uptime |
| Sales Dashboard | Pipeline, ARR, Win Rate, Deal Cycle | Daily (10 AM IST) | Salesforce Reports API | Forecast entry by reps | Updated by 10 AM same day |
| CS Dashboard | NRR, Churn, CSAT, Onboarding | Weekly (Mon 9 AM) | Gainsight + SQL query | None | Updated every Monday |
| Finance Dashboard | OPEX, Cash, Burn, Payables | Weekly (Fri) | QuickBooks / Accounting system | None | Updated every Friday |

**Automation Note**: Use tools like Tableau, Looker, Google Data Studio, or custom dashboards (Retool, Superset). 
Avoid manual dashboards; they go stale.

### 5. ALERT & THRESHOLD DEFINITIONS

Define when a metric triggers an alert to management.

**Format**:

| Metric | Green Threshold | Yellow Threshold | Red Threshold | Alert Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue vs Plan | >95% | 85-95% | <85% | Slack notification to CEO; emergency sales standup scheduled |
| NRR | >110% | 100-110% | <100% | Email to VP CS; weekly customer review scheduled |
| Cash Runway | >12 months | 6-12 months | <6 months | Email to CFO + CEO; board notification required |
| Pipeline | >1.5x month target | 1.2-1.5x | <1.2x | Email to VP Sales; deal review with exec leadership |

**Automation**: Alerts should push to Slack, email, or SMS (not require manual checking).
