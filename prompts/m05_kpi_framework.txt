## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The KPI Framework is a library of metrics that define "how we keep score." It creates:
- **North Star Metric**: The single most important metric that drives company success
- **CEO-Level KPIs**: 5-7 metrics the CEO reviews weekly (revenue, margin, churn, growth, health)
- **Department KPIs**: Function-specific metrics (sales, product, ops, finance, etc.)
- **KPI Definitions**: Standardized template for each metric (formula, frequency, owner, targets)
- **KPI Hierarchy**: Links department KPIs up to CEO KPIs to North Star

This framework is the "source of truth" for what success looks like. It feeds into M06 (MIS Dashboard), 
M07 (Reviews), M08 (Performance), and M09 (Variable Pay).

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. NORTH STAR METRIC (1 metric, company-wide)

The North Star is the ONE metric that best measures progress toward the Vision/Mission.

**Criteria**:
- Directly tied to business success (revenue, retention, impact)
- Drives all other metrics downstream
- Actionable (teams can directly influence it)
- Measurable weekly/monthly
- Understandable to all levels of organization

**Examples by Business Model**:

| Business Model | North Star | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS | Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) or ARR | Direct measure of sustainable business |
| D2C / E-commerce | Monthly Revenue or GMV | Direct revenue measure |
| Marketplace | Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) or Active Users | Liquidity and engagement |
| Professional Services | Utilization % or Project Margins | Drive profitability |
| Manufacturing | Units Produced to Plan or Gross Margin % | Drive operations and profitability |

**CEO Question**: "If we move this ONE metric in the right direction, does everything else improve?"

---

### 2. CEO-LEVEL KPIs (5-7 metrics)

CEO reviews these weekly or bi-weekly. They represent company health across key dimensions.

**Typical Dimensions** (select 5-7 for your company):

| Dimension | Sample KPI | Frequency | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue & Growth** | Monthly Revenue / ARR | Weekly | Are we growing? |
| **Profitability** | Gross Margin % / Net Margin % | Monthly | Are we efficient? |
| **Retention** | NRR % / Churn Rate | Monthly | Is our business sticky? |
| **Scale & Efficiency** | Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Monthly | Are we scaling efficiently? |
| **People & Culture** | Headcount / Attrition Rate | Monthly | Is team healthy? |
| **Cash & Finance** | Cash on Hand / Burn Rate / OPEX vs Budget | Weekly | Can we survive and invest? |
| **Product & Quality** | Feature Completion % / Bugs vs Target | Monthly | Is product roadmap on track? |

**Example CEO Dashboard** (for SaaS, 5-7 metrics):
1. **MRR**: ₹X Cr target, ₹X Cr actual, 95% of plan
2. **Gross Margin %**: 65% target, 62% actual (flag: engineering costs up)
3. **NRR %**: 110% target, 108% actual
4. **New Customer Count**: 50 target, 45 actual, 90% of plan
5. **CAC**: ₹X target, ₹X actual (2.5x LTV = healthy)
6. **Headcount**: 50 current, 55 by year-end (on plan)
7. **Cash Runway**: 24 months (sufficient)

### 4. KPI DEFINITION TABLE (STANDARDIZED TEMPLATE)

For each KPI, define consistently so multiple people calculate it the same way.

**Format** (create one row per KPI):

| KPI Name | Definition | Formula | Frequency | Owner | Annual Target | Monthly Target (Avg) | Threshold/Floor | Data Source | Green/Yellow/Red |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | Predictable revenue from active subscriptions | Sum of all active monthly subscriptions + annual contracts / 12 | Weekly | VP Sales / CFO | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr (floor) | Accounting system / Stripe | >102% on plan = Green |
| Net Revenue Retention (NRR) | % growth in cohort revenue year-over-year | (Revenue this month from [X months ago cohort]) / (Revenue [X months ago]) * 100 | Monthly | VP CS | 110% | 110% | 100% (floor = no churn) | CS platform / spreadsheet | >110% = Green |
| Gross Margin % | % of revenue remaining after COGS | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue * 100 | Monthly | CFO | 65% | 65% | 60% | Accounting system | >65% = Green |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Cost to acquire one customer | Total Sales + Marketing spend / New customers acquired | Monthly | VP Marketing | ₹X | ₹X | ₹X | Marketing analytics + Accounting |  |

**Quality Checklist for KPI Definitions**:
- [ ] Formula is clear and unambiguous
- [ ] Data source is identified (not just "we'll figure it out")
- [ ] Owner is a named role (not "team")
- [ ] Frequency is realistic (don't track hourly unless automated)
- [ ] Target is based on plan or historical trend (not arbitrary)
- [ ] Floor/threshold is defensible (below this = problem)
- [ ] Calculation methodology is documented (so no surprises)

### 5. KPI HIERARCHY (CEO → Department → Team)

Map how department KPIs ladder up to CEO KPIs to North Star.

**Example Hierarchy** (SaaS):

```
NORTH STAR: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
│
├─ CEO KPI: Revenue Growth (MoM %)
│   │
│   ├─ Sales: New MRR (new customers)
│   │   ├─ Enterprise Sales: New Enterprise MRR
│   │   └─ Mid-Market Sales: New Mid-Market MRR
│   │
│   └─ CS: NRR (expansion + retention)
│       ├─ CS: Gross Churn %
│       └─ CS: NRR by Cohort
│
├─ CEO KPI: Gross Margin %
│   │
│   └─ Finance: COGS as % Revenue
│       └─ Ops: Infrastructure Cost
│
├─ CEO KPI: CAC
│   │
│   └─ Marketing: CAC Payback Period
│       ├─ Digital Marketing: CAC (paid)
│       └─ Sales Dev: CAC (sales-assisted)
│
└─ CEO KPI: Headcount / Burn
    │
    └─ HR: Attrition %
        └─ [By department]
```

**Purpose**: If a team metric is off, you can trace it up to understand impact on CEO metrics and North Star.
