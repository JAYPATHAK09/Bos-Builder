# M02: VISION & MISSION MODULE PROMPT
## Generate Strategic Direction and Market Positioning

**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{MODULE_INPUTS}}, {{UPSTREAM_M01}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

Vision and Mission translate Purpose into strategic direction. They answer:
- Where is the company going in 10 years? (Vision - the BHAG, Big Hairy Audacious Goal)
- What does it do and for whom? (Mission - the business scope)
- What is its unique market position? (Positioning)
- What can customers count on? (Brand Promise)

These documents guide resource allocation, market entry decisions, and product roadmap.

---

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. VISION STATEMENT (1 sentence, 15-25 words, specific and measurable)

The Vision describes the company 10 years from now. It is a BHAG (Big Hairy Audacious Goal).

**Criteria**:
- Specific: Names a market position, company scale, or impact metric (not vague like "be a leader")
- Inspiring: Ambitious enough that it would excite the team
- Measurable: You could identify in 10 years whether you achieved it
- Time-bounded: Always "10-year vision" (aligns with planning horizon)
- Aligned with Purpose (from M01): Does it ladder to why the company exists?

**Examples** (Good vs. Bad):

GOOD: "Become the most trusted digital compliance platform in India, serving 50,000+ MSMEs"
- Specific (digital compliance, 50k MSMEs), measurable (customer count, market position)

GOOD: "Enable every small manufacturer in South Asia to compete globally via AI-powered supply chain tools"
- Specific (small manufacturers, South Asia, AI supply chain), inspiring

BAD: "Be a market leader" - too vague
BAD: "Grow 10x by 2035" - no qualitative vision
BAD: "Transform the industry" - generic

---

### 2. MISSION STATEMENT (Max 2 sentences, answer: WHAT, FOR WHOM, HOW)

Mission is narrower than Vision. It defines the business scope and operating philosophy.

**Template**: 
"We [WHAT we do] for [FOR WHOM - target customer], so that [IMPACT/HOW they benefit]."

**Criteria**:
- Scope clarity: Clear what the company does and doesn't do (helps with "No" decisions)
- Customer-centric: Focused on customer benefit, not internal activity
- Realistic: Achievable in 3-5 years (shorter time horizon than Vision)
- Actionable: Guides product and market decisions

**Example Missions**:

"We provide AI-powered payroll and HR compliance software for Indian MSMEs (50-250 employees), so 
they can save 20 hours/month on compliance and focus on growth."

"We design and deliver enterprise software training programs for corporations in South Asia, 
so that their technical teams ship software faster and with fewer defects."

**Common Mistakes**:
- Too broad ("We provide digital solutions") - could be anything
- Too internal ("We build great products") - doesn't mention customer benefit
- Confusing mission with vision ("Become the #1 provider by 2035") - that's vision
- Vague customer ("Serve businesses everywhere") - no focus

---

### 3. POSITIONING STATEMENT (1 sentence, 20-30 words)

Positioning explains why this company is different in the market and why a specific customer 
should choose it over alternatives.

**Template**:
"For [TARGET CUSTOMER], [COMPANY NAME] is [CATEGORY], that [UNIQUE BENEFIT]. Unlike [COMPETITOR], 
we [KEY DIFFERENTIATOR]."

**Example Positioning**:

"For CFOs in India managing remote teams, TallyConnect is a cloud-first payroll platform that 
integrates with Tally and complies with all local GST rules. Unlike legacy on-premise solutions, 
we update tax rules monthly without requiring manual system updates."

**Key Elements**:
- Target customer: Who specifically (not everyone)
- Category: What business are you in (payroll software, consulting, marketplace, etc.)
- Unique benefit: Why does it matter to this customer specifically
- Differentiation: Why can't they get this elsewhere

---

### 4. BRAND PROMISE (2-3 sentences)

Brand Promise is what customers can ALWAYS count on, every time.

**Criteria**:
- Specific to your company (not generic)
- Deliverable consistently (you can reliably meet it)
- Valued by customers (they would choose you for this reason)

**Examples**:

"Our software is live and available 99.9% of the time. If we go down, you get service credits 
equal to that downtime. Our customer support responds within 2 hours, even at 2 AM."

"We never sell a product that isn't a perfect fit for your use case, even if it means walking 
away from revenue. We audit your existing tools first and tell you if you don't need to buy."

## ALIGNMENT WITH M01

If {{UPSTREAM_M01}} is available, ensure:
- Vision and Mission honor the company's Purpose (why it exists)
- The market position doesn't contradict core Values
- Positioning reflects company's non-negotiables (e.g., if "never compromise quality," positioning 
  shouldn't be "lowest cost")

**Consultant's Question**: Does the Vision get us closer to living out the Purpose?
