**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{MODULE_INPUTS}}, {{UPSTREAM_M03}}, {{INDUSTRY_TYPE}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The Annual Operating Plan translates Year 1 of the 3-Year Strategic Plan into actionable monthly, 
quarterly, and annual goals with accountability, budget, and operating rhythm.

An AOP answers:
- What are the annual goals and KPIs for the company?
- What is the quarterly roadmap (Q1-Q4)?
- What are department-specific plans and ownership?
- How much budget is allocated? To what?
- What is the operating rhythm (weekly/monthly/quarterly reviews)?
- What are the top 10 risks and mitigations?

An AOP is a "living document" reviewed quarterly and updated in December for the next fiscal year.

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-2 pages)

A concise overview of the AOP for the CFO/Board.

**Includes**:
- FY Headline: "FY2025-26: Scale Product to 500 customers, launch India operations"
- Revenue target: ₹XX Cr
- Gross margin target: Y%
- Headcount: Current → Target
- 3-5 strategic priorities (pulled from M03)
- Top 3 risks

---

### 2. ANNUAL GOALS WITH KPIs

Define 5-7 company-level annual goals (linked to M03 strategic themes).

**Format**:

| Goal # | Goal | Owner | Annual Target | Quarterly Milestones (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4) | KPI Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reach ₹XX Cr revenue | CFO/CEO | ₹XX Cr | Q1: ₹XX Cr; Q2: ₹XX Cr; Q3: ₹XX Cr; Q4: ₹XX Cr | Monthly revenue tracking |
| 2 | Launch product in 3 new geographies | COO/Product | 3 geos | Q2: 1 geo; Q3: 2 geos; Q4: 3 geos | Geo expansion log |

**Quality Criteria**:
- Each goal is SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Owner is a named role (not "team")
- Quarterly milestones are checkpoints (not just linear division of annual target)
- Every goal is tied to a KPI that will be tracked (links to M05)

---

### 3. DEPARTMENT-WISE PLANS (Auto-selected by Industry Type)

Generate function-specific plans for key departments. Use {{INDUSTRY_TYPE}} to select relevant functions:

**For B2B/SaaS** (typical):
- Sales (ARR targets, new customer acquisition, pipeline management)
- Product & Engineering (feature roadmap, technical debt, quality metrics)
- Customer Success (NRR, churn, onboarding velocity)
- Finance & Admin (headcount budget, OPEX management, compliance)
- Marketing (MQL targets, brand initiatives, content roadmap)

**For D2C/E-commerce** (typical):
- Direct Sales (revenue, order volume, AOV)
- Marketing & Growth (CAC, ROAS, channel mix)
- Operations (fulfillment, returns, inventory)
- Finance (COGS, margins, cash flow)
- Product (UX improvements, feature launches)

**For Manufacturing** (typical):
- Sales (volume, mix, account management)
- Operations (production capacity, quality, supply chain)
- Finance (cost per unit, margin improvement)
- HR (talent, safety, process compliance)

**For Professional Services** (typical):
- Delivery (utilization, project margins, quality)
- Sales & BD (new client acquisition, AUM, pricing)
- Finance (project accounting, cost management, cash)
- Talent & Culture (hiring, bench management)

**Format for Each Dept**:

| Department | Primary Goals (3-4) | Owner | Budget Allocation | Key Risks | Link to M05 KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | ARR: ₹XX Cr; New Cust: X; Pipeline: 3x | VP Sales | ₹XX L | Pricing pressure; retention | Revenue, NRR, CAC |

**Detailed Subgoals** (one per department):
- Specific initiatives, headcount plans, tool/tech investments
- Q1-Q4 roadmap (what ships when)
- Success metrics unique to that function

### 4. QUARTERLY ROADMAP (Q1-Q4)

Translate annual initiatives into quarterly execution plan.

**Format**:

| Phase | Months | Key Deliverables | Dependencies | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Apr-Jun) | Jan-Mar or Q1 | - Launch SME pricing tier (Product) | Market research (completed Q4) | Product |
| | | - Hire 2 sales engineers (Sales) | | HR/VP Sales |
| | | - Build customer feedback loop (CS) | | VP CS |
| Q2 (Jul-Sep) | Apr-Jun or Q2 | [Continue...] | | |

**Quality Notes**:
- Dependencies must be identified (if Q2 depends on Q1, flag it)
- Owner is a named role
- Deliverables should be verifiable (not vague like "improve product")

| Category | Budget (₹L) | % of Revenue | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **People Costs** | ₹XXX L | 45% | Salary, benefits, hiring (X new roles) |
| **Technology & Tools** | ₹XX L | 8% | Cloud infra, software licenses, AI tools |

| **Operations** | ₹XX L | 12% | Facilities, compliance, outsourced services |
| **R&D / Product Development** | ₹XX L | 15% | Engineering, product design, R&D |
| **Admin & Finance** | ₹XX L | 5% | Accounting, legal, HR systems |
| **Contingency (5%)** | ₹XX L | 5% | Buffer for opportunities and emergencies |
| **TOTAL OPEX** | ₹XXX L | 100% | |

**Department Budget Breakdown** (optional detail):

| Department | Annual Budget | Headcount | Budget per HC | Key Investments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | ₹XXX L | 10 | ₹XX L/person | Senior hire, infra upgrade |
| Sales | ₹XX L | 4 | ₹XX L/person | Comp, travel, tools |

---

### 6. RISK REGISTER (Top 10 Risks)

Identify major risks and mitigation plans.

**Format**:

| Risk # | Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Key engineer departure | Talent | Medium | High | Competitive comp review; knowledge docs; mentor junior | CTO | If anyone indicates planning departure |
| 2 | Slower customer acquisition due to market slowdown | Market | Medium | High | Diversify sales channels; improve unit economics; expand TAM | VP Sales | If pipeline growth <20% vs. forecast |

**Risk Categories**: Talent, Market, Product, Technology, Financial, Compliance, Competitive, Operational

---

### 7. OPERATING RHYTHM (Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly Cadences)

Define how the org will review progress and stay aligned.

**Format**:

| Cadence | Meeting | Participants | Duration | Agenda | Success Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Ops Standup | Dept heads | 30 min | Top 3 priorities; blockers; risk flags | All blockers resolved same week or escalated |
| **Monthly** | Business Review (MBR) | Leadership team | 90 min | KPI review (vs. plan); wins; risks; next month forecast | RAG status on all goals; actions assigned |
| **Quarterly** | Strategic Review (QBR) | Full leadership | 4 hours | Strategic progress; market insights; org health; next quarter plan | Decision on Q+1 priorities |
| **Annual** | Annual Planning | CEO + Board | 1-2 days | Full AOP for next year; 3-year plan refresh | New AOP published by [date] |

**Note**: This content feeds into M07 (Review System).

### 8. KEY SUCCESS INDICATORS & METRICS

List the 5-10 metrics that matter most for AOP execution.

**Format**:

| Metric | Owner | Frequency | Annual Target | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | CFO | Monthly | ₹XX Cr | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr | ₹X Cr |
| Gross Margin % | CFO | Monthly | 60% | 55% | 58% | 60% | 62% |
| NRR % | VP CS | Monthly | 110% | 105% | 108% | 110% | 112% |
| Employee Satisfaction | HR | Quarterly | 8.0/10 | — | 7.5 | — | 8.0 |

**Note**: These become detailed KPI definitions in M05.
