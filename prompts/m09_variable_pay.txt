## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The Variable Pay Framework defines how bonuses and incentives work. It answers:
- Who is eligible for variable pay? (Not everyone)
- How much can they earn? (As % of salary by level)
- What KPIs drive bonuses? (Company, department, individual)
- What's the payout curve? (How fast does it ramp?)
- What's the gate condition? (Does company have to hit minimum?)

A well-designed variable pay program:
- **Motivates** key behaviors (aligned with KPIs)
- **Rewards** performance (clear upside opportunity)
- **Is Transparent** (everyone knows the formula)
- **Is Achievable** (targets are realistic, not demotivating)
- **Scales with Company** (doesn't break cash flow)

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. ELIGIBILITY BY BAND

Define who gets variable pay and how much.

**Typical Structure** (by employee level):

| Band | Role Level | Examples | Eligibility | Variable Pay Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Band 1** | Individual Contributors (L0-L2) | Associate, Specialist, Senior IC | No variable pay | 0% of base salary |
| **Band 2** | Junior Managers (L3) | Team Lead, Junior Manager | Optional; recommend yes | 10% of base salary |
| **Band 3** | Managers (L4) | Manager, Senior Manager | Yes (required) | 20% of base salary |
| **Band 4** | Senior Management (L5) | Director, Head of Function | Yes (required) | 30% of base salary |
| **Band 5** | Executive (L6+) | VP, CEO | Yes (required) | 40-50% of base salary |

**Recommendation** (for Indian startups/SMEs):
- **Band 1-2**: Optional variable pay (or none); helps keep fixed costs down in early stage
- **Band 3+**: Mandatory variable pay; aligns incentives at leadership level
- **CEO**: 40-50% bonus opportunity (tied to company KPIs)

**Rationale**: Only senior enough people should have discretion over KPIs; junior contributors 
are mostly execution.

---

### 2. KPI LINKAGE (Which KPIs Drive Variable Pay)

Define which metrics trigger bonus payout.

**Typical Structure**:

**Company-Level Gate** (all variable pay subject to this):
- Minimum company performance threshold (e.g., "Achieve ≥80% of revenue target")
- If company misses gate, no one gets bonus (or reduced bonus)
- Rationale: Aligns everyone to company success; prevents bonusing individuals while company fails

**Department-Level KPIs** (drives portion of variable pay):
- 30-50% of bonus driven by department KPIs (e.g., Sales team bonused on ARR + NRR)
- Allows departments to optimize for their role in company success
- Example: Sales 50% on new revenue, 30% on NRR, 20% on profitability

**Individual-Level KPIs** (drives portion of variable pay):
- 30-50% of bonus driven by individual KPI scorecard
- Rewards personal contribution
- Example: Sales rep bonused on personal quota, team collaboration, customer satisfaction

**Example Bonus Structure** (Sales Team):

| Component | Weight | Driver | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Company Gate** | 100% threshold | Revenue vs Budget | Must achieve ≥90% of company revenue target or no bonus |
| **Department KPI: ARR Growth** | 40% | New + Expansion ARR | Weighted average of team ARR vs. target |
| **Department KPI: Profitability** | 10% | Gross Margin % | Company gross margin vs. target |
| **Individual KPI: New Customers** | 35% | Quota attainment | Individual quota vs. target |
| **Individual KPI: NRR Impact** | 10% | Customer retention & expansion | Individual's customer NRR ≥ 110% |
| **Behavioral (from PMS)** | 5% | Rating | Bonus modifier: Rating 4+ = 1.0x; Rating 3 = 0.8x; Rating 2 = 0x |

**Bonus Payout Example** (assuming all targets met):

If employee has ₹10L base salary:
- Variable pay opportunity = 20% × ₹10L = ₹2L potential
- If all KPIs hit 100%: Payout = ₹2L
- If KPIs hit 80% of target: Payout = ₹2L × 80% = ₹1.6L
- If company misses gate: Payout = ₹0 (no bonus)

### 3. PAYOUT CURVE (How $ varies with performance)

Define how payout ramps with KPI achievement.

**Typical Payout Curve**:

| Performance Level | Achievement | Payout % |
|---|---|---|
| **Below Threshold** | <80% of target | 0% |
| **Threshold** | 80% of target | 25% of max bonus |
| **Target** | 100% of target | 100% of max bonus |
| **Above Target** | 100-120% of target | 100-150% of max bonus (linear) |
| **Exceeds** | >120% of target | 150% of max bonus (capped) |

**Graphical Representation**:

```
Payout %
150% |              X (>120% achievement)
     |            /
100% |          X (100% achievement = target)
     |        /
25%  |      X (80% = threshold)
0%   |____________________________
     0%    80%    100%   120%    150%
                Achievement %
```

**Rationale**:
- Below 80% = no payout (motivates minimum performance)
- 80-100% = linear increase (encourages closing the gap)
- 100-120% = continued linear increase (rewards outperformance)
- >120% = capped at 150% (prevents runaway payouts if target set too low)

**Alternative for High-Risk Roles** (e.g., Sales):
- 0% below threshold
- Accelerated curve above target (e.g., 150% payout at 120% achievement)
- Rationale: Sales exceeding target by 20% should be rewarded more aggressively

---

### 4. GATE CONDITIONS (Company Must Hit Minimum)

Define when bonuses are paid out at all.

**Typical Gate Conditions** (pick one or combine):

| Gate | Trigger | Implication | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue Gate | Company achieves ≥90% of revenue target | No bonus if company misses revenue | Ensures profitable growth |
| Profit Gate | Company achieves ≥80% of net margin target | No bonus if company is unprofitable | Aligns with company health |
| Cash Gate | Company maintains >6 months cash runway | No bonus if cash is critical | Prevents bonusing away liquidity |
| Combined | Revenue AND Profit AND Cash gates all must pass | No bonus unless all 3 met | Most stringent; ensures all dimensions healthy |

**Recommendation** (for Indian startups/SMEs):
- **Early stage (bootstrapped/pre-raise)**: Revenue gate (top line matters most)
- **Growth stage (funded)**: Revenue + Profit gates (avoiding unit economics trap)
- **Mature stage (profitable)**: Revenue + Profit gates (cash usually healthy)

**Example Gate Logic**:

"All variable pay bonuses are subject to company achieving ≥90% of annual revenue target. 
If company achieves <90% of target, all bonuses are reduced by 50%. If company achieves 
<80% of target, no bonuses are paid."

| Role | Base Salary | Variable Opportunity | Bonus % Calculation | Actual Payout |
