---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The Sales Tracker is the operational dashboard for monitoring revenue health in real-time. 
It answers:
- Are we on track to hit monthly/quarterly/annual revenue targets?
- What is pipeline visibility into future months?
- How are individual sales team members performing vs. quota?
- What is the forecast for the next 2 months?
- What are the key leading indicators of future revenue?

A well-designed sales tracker:
- **Real-time**: Updated daily or weekly (not monthly)
- **Actionable**: Shows what needs immediate attention
- **Transparent**: Everyone knows where deals are
- **Forecasted**: Provides visibility 60+ days ahead
- **Tied to KPIs**: Links to M05 sales KPIs

### 3. 2-MONTH ROLLING FORECAST (Methodology and Table)

Provide 60-day forward visibility with high confidence.

**Methodology**:
1. **Month 1 Forecast** = Very high confidence (95%+)
   - Only includes deals with >80% probability of close
   - Based on committed customer interactions
   - Typically 80-90% of target

2. **Month 2 Forecast** = Medium confidence (60-70%)
   - Includes deals with >50% probability of close
   - Based on proposal status and customer engagement
   - Typically 50-70% of target

3. **Month 3+ ** = Low confidence (<50%)
   - Pipeline-based forecast using historical conversion
   - Not included in "rolling forecast" (shown separately as "pipeline")

**Rolling Forecast Table** (Updated weekly):

| Forecast Period | Month 1 (May) | Month 2 (Jun) | Month 3+ (Jul+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue Target** | ₹80L | ₹80L | ₹80L+ (pipeline) |
| **High Confidence Deals (>80% prob)** | ₹75L | ₹50L | — |
| **Medium Confidence Deals (50-80% prob)** | ₹10L | ₹35L | — |
| **Pipeline (>50% prob)** | — | — | ₹120L (3.2x target) |
| **Total Forecast** | **₹85L** | **₹85L** | **₹120L (expected)** |
| **% of Target** | **106%** | **106%** | **150%** |
| **Confidence Level** | **95%** | **70%** | **60%** |

### 6. KEY LEADING INDICATORS (Predictive Metrics)

Track metrics that predict future revenue (not lagging revenue itself).

**Leading Indicators** (updated weekly/bi-weekly):

| Indicator | Definition | Target | Current | Status | Implication for Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qualified Leads (30+ days old)** | Leads that have had 2+ interactions | 50+ | 48 | Yellow | On track for M+1; M+2 may short |
| **Active Pipeline (Move Last 30 Days)** | Deals that advanced stage in last month | 15+ | 12 | Yellow | Fewer deals advancing; velocity slowing |
| **Sales Activity (Calls/Demos)** | # of qualified customer interactions | 200+ | 185 | Yellow | Activity down; may impact future pipeline |
| **Customer Response Rate** | % of outbound emails with reply | >20% | 18% | Yellow | Email content or list quality issue |
| **Avg Sales Cycle (Recent Deals)** | Days from first meeting to close | 45 days | 52 days | Red | Extending; decision-making getting slower |

**Dashboard Update Frequency**: Weekly (Monday AM) to sales team

**Action Triggers**:
- If Qualified Leads <40: emergency outbound campaign
- If Sales Cycle >60 days: deal review with leadership; identify blockers
- If Activity <170: coaching conversations with underperformers
