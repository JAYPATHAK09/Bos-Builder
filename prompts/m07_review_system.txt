## Define Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual Review Cadences with Agendas

**Input Variables**: {{COMPANY_PROFILE}}, {{UPSTREAM_M05}}, {{UPSTREAM_M06}}

---

## CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

The Business Review System is the operational rhythm of the organization. It's where:
- Progress against goals is measured (KPIs reviewed)
- Problems are surfaced and solved (escalations, risks)
- Decisions are made and communicated
- Accountability is maintained

A well-designed review system has:
- **Cadences**: Weekly standup, monthly business review, quarterly strategic review, annual planning
- **Agendas**: Structured, time-boxed, clear outcomes
- **Participants**: Right people in each meeting (not everyone in everything)
- **Escalation Rules**: Clear criteria for when something needs higher-level attention
- **Action Tracking**: How commitments are captured and followed up

---

## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. WEEKLY OPERATIONS STANDUP (30 min, Dept Heads)

The weekly sync keeps leadership aligned on top priorities and blockers.

**Attendees**:
- CEO/Founder
- Department heads (VP Sales, VP Eng, VP CS, VP Marketing, CFO)
- If large org: include Ops leader or COO

**Format** (30 minutes total):

| Agenda Item | Duration | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pulse Check**: CEO shares 1-2 week priorities | 2 min | CEO | Alignment on what matters this week |
| **Top 3 Blockers**: Each dept head shares top blocker; ask for help | 15 min | Dept heads | Action items captured; blockers escalated |
| **Key Metrics Snapshot**: Quick review of CEO KPIs (red/yellow/green) | 5 min | CFO | Identify metric misses early |
| **Risk Flags**: Any emerging risks or decisions needed | 5 min | All | Escalation to CEO for urgent decisions |
| **Next Week Preview**: What's coming next week | 3 min | CEO | Prepare team for upcoming events |

**Success Criteria**:
- [ ] Meeting ends on time (hard stop at 30 min)
- [ ] All blockers are assigned to someone (not just discussed)
- [ ] CEO is aware of key blockers before they derail week
- [ ] Attendance >90% (no-show is red flag for culture)

**Escalation Rules**:
- If red on revenue → CEO drives sales leadership meeting same day
- If red on cash → Emergency finance review
- If engineering blocker → CTO + CEO resolve in 24 hours

---

### 2. MONTHLY BUSINESS REVIEW (MBR) (90 min, Leadership)

The MBR is the primary review rhythm. It's where KPIs are assessed, actions are reviewed, and next month is planned.

**Attendees**:
- CEO/Founder
- CFO / Finance lead
- Department heads (Sales, Product, CS, Marketing, Ops, HR)
- Optional: Board advisor or investor (once/quarter)

**Agenda** (90 minutes):

| Agenda Item | Duration | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CEO Context**: Recap of priorities; market updates | 5 min | CEO | Shared context for month review |
| **Financial Snapshot**: Revenue, gross margin, OPEX vs budget, cash position | 10 min | CFO | Green/yellow/red on financials |
| **KPI Review**: Review all CEO KPIs vs plan; deep-dive on red metrics | 30 min | CFO + metric owners | Identify root cause of misses; action items assigned |
| **Department Updates**: Each dept head shares: wins, challenges, next month priorities | 20 min | Dept heads | Transparency on progress; identify cross-dept dependencies |
| **Risk & Issue Log**: Review top 5-10 risks; any new risks; mitigation updates | 15 min | CEO + Risk owner | Updated risk register |
| **Next Month Plan**: Priorities, key deliverables, milestones for next 30 days | 10 min | Dept heads | Clear 1-month roadmap |

**MBR Metrics Agenda** (30-min deep dive):

**Table Format** (shows all KPIs):

| KPI | Target | Actual | % Plan | RAG | Owner | Root Cause (if red/yellow) | Action Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Revenue | ₹5.5 Cr | ₹5.2 Cr | 95% | Yellow | VP Sales | 2 large deals slipped to next month | Weekly pipeline review; deal coaching |
| Gross Margin % | 65% | 62% | 95% | Yellow | CFO | Eng headcount growth outpacing revenue | Headcount freeze after Q2 hiring |
| NRR % | 110% | 108% | 98% | Green | VP CS | Expansion revenue lower than forecast | Customer expansion campaign in Q2 |
| CAC | ₹X | ₹X | 100% | Green | VP Marketing | On track | No action needed |

**Action Item Tracking** (from MBR):

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conduct engineering cost review | CFO | End of month | In progress | Deciding between headcount vs contractors |
| Run deal coaching for slipped deals | VP Sales | Next week | — | Weekly cadence starting next Monday |

### 3. QUARTERLY BUSINESS REVIEW (QBR) (4 hours, Leadership + Board)

The QBR is a deeper strategic review. It's often held with board members or advisors and shapes the next quarter.

**Attendees**:
- CEO/Founder
- CFO
- All department heads
- Board members or key advisors
- Optional: Key customers for feedback

**Agenda** (4 hours, with break):

| Agenda Item | Duration | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CEO State of Business**: 10-min overview of quarter performance vs plan | 10 min | CEO | Alignment on strategic health |
| **Financial & KPI Review**: Deep-dive on all metrics, trends, 3-quarter view | 45 min | CFO + owners | Identify patterns; strategic implications |
| **Initiative Deep-Dives**: Review progress on 3-5 strategic initiatives (from M03/M04) | 60 min | Initiative owners | Assess on-track/at-risk/completed status |
| **Customer & Market Insights**: Voice of customer, competitive intel, market trends | 30 min | VP Sales + VP CS | Market context for next quarter plans |
| **Org Health**: Headcount, attrition, engagement, culture updates | 15 min | HR | People health check |
| **Risk Register Review**: Update top 10 risks; any new emerging risks | 20 min | CEO + Risk owner | Mitigation status update |
| **Next Quarter Plan**: Priorities, key deliverables, initiatives for Q+1 | 30 min | Dept heads | Clear 3-month roadmap with milestones |

**QBR Format** (slide deck):

Typically:
- Slide 1: CEO letter (1 page: performance vs plan, key learnings, Q+1 priorities)
- Slide 2: KPI dashboard (CEO KPIs with 3-quarter trend)
- Slides 3-5: Department deep-dives (wins, challenges, Q+1 plans)
- Slide 6: Risk register with updates
- Slide 7: Q+1 priorities and deliverables

---

### 4. ANNUAL PLANNING CYCLE (2-3 days, Leadership + Board)

The annual planning session creates the next year's AOP (M04). Usually held in November-December 
for April-March fiscal year.

**Attendees**:
- CEO/Founder
- CFO
- All department heads
- Board members (usually 1-2 days with them)

**Agenda** (multi-day offsite):

| Day | Session | Duration | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Environmental Scan: Market, competition, customer trends | 2 hours | CEO + Market lead | Market context for planning |
| | Strategic Themes for Next Year: Refine M03 strategy for upcoming year | 3 hours | CEO + Strategy | 3-5 strategic themes for FY |
| **Day 2** | Financial Targets: Revenue, margin, headcount targets for FY | 2 hours | CFO + Dept leads | Annual targets by dept |
| | Department Deep-Dive Planning: Each dept plans initiatives, budget, KPIs | 4 hours | Dept heads | Detailed department plans |
| | Risk Identification & Mitigation: Full planning team | 1.5 hours | CEO + Risk team | Comprehensive risk register |
| **Day 3** | Draft AOP Assembly: Finance pulls together budget and targets | 3 hours | CFO + Ops | Integrated AOP draft |
| | Board Presentation Prep: Build investor/board update | 1.5 hours | CEO + CFO | Board-ready presentation |

**Output**: Complete AOP (M04) ready for board approval.

### 6. ACTION TRACKING SYSTEM

How actions are captured, tracked, and followed up to completion.

**Format** (Shared Action Log):

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Priority | Status | Notes | Follow-up Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fix engineering cost structure | CFO | Mar 31 | High | In progress | Evaluating headcount vs. outsourcing | Feb 15 MBR |
| Launch SME pricing tier | VP Product | Feb 15 | High | On track | 80% complete; QA in progress | Weekly standup |
| Hire customer success manager | HR | Jan 31 | High | At Risk | Got 15 applications; extending search | Hiring update in MBR |

**Rules**:
- Every action has a named owner (not "team")
- Due dates are specific (not "soon")
- Status is updated in the action log before each review meeting
- Red/yellow/green status is clear
- Actions older than 30 days overdue should be escalated

**Tool**: Use Google Sheets, Asana, Jira, or Notion (pick one tool; live shared doc).
